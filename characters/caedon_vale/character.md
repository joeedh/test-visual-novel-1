A nineteen-year-old first-year at the Cog-College, male. Caedon is the first channeler in a generation to test strong in all five Threads, a fact he keeps hidden. Earnest, quick-witted, and stubborn, he arrives from a modest Amberline artificer family with more curiosity than caution. Dark tousled hair, warm brown eyes, an ink-stained Loomwright-blue student coat over a brass-buttoned waistcoat. He channels tentatively, still unsure whether to fear the Rust or ride it.
