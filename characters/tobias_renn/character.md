name: Tobias Renn
---

A student at the Cog-College and Caedon's closest friend. Tobias is a broad-shouldered, easygoing Ferran of common stock, the son of a furnace-tender, who won his place through raw Sela talent rather than birth. Warm, loyal, and quick with a joke, he wears his brass-and-white livery slightly askew and never quite trusts the Governor bolted to his forearm. He dreams of engineering great aether furnaces but fears burning out. Practical, generous, and steadier than he lets on.
