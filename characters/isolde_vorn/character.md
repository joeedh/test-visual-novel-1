name: Isolde Vorn
---

A student of the Cog-College and daughter of the Grey Principality of Vorn's devout prince-regent. Isolde is Sela-strong, cold and precise in manner, raised amid the Assayers' austere piety and the tight leashing of channeling. She wears unmarked grey and iron, carries herself with rigid discipline, and hides a fierce curiosity about the very ungoverned power her homeland forbids. Pale, sharp-featured, dark-haired, with grey eyes that miss nothing. Torn between duty to Vorn and the pull of forbidden knowledge.
