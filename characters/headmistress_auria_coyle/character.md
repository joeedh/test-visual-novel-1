name: Headmistress Auria Coyle
---

The headmistress of the Cog-College, a Regulen Concord veteran in her sixties. Silver-haired and iron-spined, she wears the brass-and-white livery of the Brass Wardens with a licensed Governor bolted to her forearm. Sela-strong and utterly disciplined, she believes measured power is the only safe power and runs the College by the letter of Concord law. Beneath the stern exterior she quietly shelters talented students the Assayers might otherwise sever. Palette: brass, slate-grey, bone-white.
