name: Professor Silas Thorn
---

A Regulen Concord master and professor of Governor-craft at the Cog-College. Sela-strong, exacting, and brass-gauntleted, he teaches the metering and regulation of the Weave. Middle-aged, silver-templed, with ink-stained fingers and a valve-etched brass forearm Governor he never removes. Believes measured power is the only safe power, and distrusts anything that smells of the Kiln-Sworn.
