name: Ember Kellan
---

A fierce, ember-eyed Kiln-Sworn prospect at the Cog-College, born in the Marches of Cindral. Ember is Vash-strong and refuses to fear the Rust, channeling raw and wild where others meter themselves. Ash-grey and ember-orange are her colors; faint rust-scarring traces one forearm like a badge of defiance. Passionate, reckless, and loyal to a fault, she scorns the Concord's licensed caution and dreams of forge-mastery. Beneath the bravado she fears burning out young, as her kind do. Palette leans ash-grey, ember-orange, and iron.
