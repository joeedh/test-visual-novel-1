name: Seraphine Halcyon
---

A poised, indigo-and-silver-clad student of the Cog-College, secretly of Halcyon Reach's royal line and drawn to the Loomwrights' subtle Spirit-weaving. Sharp-witted, diplomatic, and burdened by expectation. She braids many Threads with delicate precision and keeps the oldest questions of the Weave close to her heart. Slender, with silver-blond hair and pale grey eyes.
