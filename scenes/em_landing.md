EXT. VASHAN BORDERLAND - DAY

[[line: L1]]
The Vashan Borderland is a harsh, scrubbed-open country of red dust and wind-carved rock. No forge for a hundred miles to shelter a Kiln-Sworn. Ember's raw power, typically a shield, here becomes a beacon, drawing unwanted attention across the vast, exposed horizon. We are hunted, but she pushes forward, her eyes bright with a fierce, weary resolve.

[[line: L2]]
Between escapes, as we huddle in the sparse shelter of dead telegraph lines, she lets slip, never as complaint, how little time she believes the Rust will leave her. The bloom on her arm is not just a mark of power, but a ticking clock.

[[line: L3]]
The memory of the executed pair lands on me like a warning about her, a cold premonition. I refuse to lose her to this world, or to herself.
