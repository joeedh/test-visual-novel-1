[[line: L8]]
The college infirmary is hushed and sterile. The soft, clean aether-lamp light does little to soothe the sting of our injuries.

[[line: L9]]
(Ember path) Ember is here, her arm being treated for a deeper bloom of Rust. I overhear whispers among the Healers: the Dominion's proprietary claim on her Vash strength, the Ferran distrust of ungoverned power.

[[line: L10]]
(Isolde path) Isolde quietly observes the infirmary's careful records, her key-pendant clutched tight. Vorn's watchers are always near. The Assayers' reach around her feels palpable.

[[line: L11]]
(Seraphine path) Seraphine speaks in low tones with a college dignitary, her poise unwavering. I catch fragments of talk about rival houses, treaty intrigue, old pacts and new betrayals.

[[line: L12]]
(Wren path) Wren sits apart, vigilant, her gloved hands still. The Assayer hunt for the unlicensed, for the Ashen Circle, feels closer now, a tangible threat.

[[line: L13]]
(Undivided path) I feel the pull of all four conflicts. Ember's sentence, Isolde's faith, Seraphine's courts, Wren's fugitive life. I am drawn into all of them.

[[line: L14]]
The ambush has pulled me into a conflict far larger than a college test. The world has irrevocably shifted.
