INT. INFIRMARY - NIGHT

[[next: c09_woods_training]]
[[nextline: 8]]

[[line: L1]]
The college infirmary is hushed, sterile. The soft, clean aether-lamp light does little to soothe the sting of our injuries, or the cold dread in our hearts. Pip and Gideon nurse minor cuts; Tobias is already asleep, exhausted.

[[line: L2]]
Ember is here, her arm being treated for a deeper bloom of Rust. The Healers watch her with a wary respect, and I overhear whispers of the Dominion's proprietary claim on her Vash strength, and the Ferran distrust of ungoverned power. I feel myself pulled into her conflict.

[[line: L3]]
(Route colour for Isolde): Isolde is quietly observing the infirmary's careful records, her key-pendant clutched tight. Vorn's watchers are always near, and the Assayers' reach around her feels palpable. The questions she's asking, the subtle defiance in her eyes, draw me to her intricate world of devotion and doubt.

[[line: L4]]
(Route colour for Seraphine): Seraphine is speaking in low tones with a college dignitary, her poise unwavering even after the ambush. I catch fragments of talk about rival houses and treaty intrigue, about old pacts and new betrayals. She navigates these currents with a practiced ease that pulls me into her orbit.

[[line: L5]]
(Route colour for Wren): Wren sits apart, vigilant, her gloved hands still. The Assayer hunt for the unlicensed, for the Ashen Circle, feels closer now, a tangible threat. Her quiet defiance, her refusal to be caged, makes me want to understand her world, to shield her from it.

[[line: L6]]
(Undivided colour): I feel the pull of all four conflicts. Ember's sentence, Isolde's faith, Seraphine's courts, Wren's fugitive life. I am drawn into all of them, refusing to pick a side, or a heart, for any one of them. The choice feels impossible, and perhaps, unnecessary.

[[line: L7]]
The ambush has pulled me, irrevocably, into a conflict far larger than a college test. My path, and the world's, has irrevocably shifted.
