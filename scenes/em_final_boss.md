[[line: L1]]
The Inner Sanctum of the Organization. A cavernous engine-cathedral, blinding aether-core glare, and deep shadows dancing on catwalks. This is it: the final assault. Our converged coalition, Ember at its fiery heart, storms the core, facing down the orchestrator of the world's chaos.

[[line: L2]]
Ember unleashes a torrent of Vash-strong current, raw and unmetered, a living storm. The Rust on her arm glows, then spreads, but she pushes through the pain, fueled by defiance and a desperate will to live. Allies from across the fragmented world fight alongside us, a testament to the coalition we built.

[[line: L3]]
The battle is a maelstrom of weaves and steel. The enemy boss, a figure cloaked in shadow and twisted aether, falls beneath the combined might of our forces. The monstrous machinery of their operation grinds to a halt, the sickly green glow finally fading.

[[line: L4]]
Silence descends, broken only by the ragged breaths of the survivors. I turn to Ember, my heart in my throat. The Rust has climbed further, but her eyes meet mine, unyielding. She is alive. We did it. We won.
