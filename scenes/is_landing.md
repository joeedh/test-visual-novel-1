[[line: L1]]
The Grey Principality of Vorn is a cold northern land of grey stone and iron. Cliff-perched devotional houses and shrine-roads dominate the landscape, where the Assayers' writ runs absolute and channeling is leashed to piety. We land here, in Isolde's homeland, a place whose methods she knows intimately, and which now cuts both ways.

[[line: L2]]
Her grasp of Assayer methods and Vorn devotional networks keeps us alive, helping us evade patrols and navigate the treacherous terrain. Her key-pendant, a symbol of her devotion, opens doors it should not, revealing hidden passages and secrets within the rigid order.

[[line: L3]]
As we delve deeper, we uncover a terrifying truth: the very order Isolde was raised to serve is deeply implicated in the world's collapse. The faith she held so dear is tainted, a weapon turned against its own people.

[[line: L4]]
The news of the executed pair lands on her like a verdict on her own faith, a stark reminder of the brutality inherent in the system she once revered. Her disciplined facade cracks, revealing a simmering doubt. I feel myself drawn into her struggle, her quiet rebellion.
