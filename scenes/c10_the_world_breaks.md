[[line: L1]]
The Great Assembly Hall is grim, hushed. Headmistress Coyle stands at the rostrum, flanked by the professors, their faces etched with a new, terrible urgency. The cheerful banners of the Orders seem out of place, mocking the dread that fills the hall.

HEADMISTRESS AURIA COYLE
[[line: L2]]
Students, the geopolitical situation has deteriorated catastrophically. The Governors have fallen silent. Strategic weaves, once thought impossible, are being deployed.

PROFESSOR MIRABEL QUIST
[[line: L3]]
Training will intensify. You are no longer merely students; you are the last line. Every lesson, every skill you have acquired, will now be tested in ways we had hoped you would never know.

[[line: L4]]
Panic ripples through the cohort. The carefree days of the college are over, shattered by the weight of a world breaking apart. Dread and urgency are palpable. Our instructors prepare us for a war we never asked for, a fight we don't understand.

[[line: L5]]
My eyes meet Ember's, then Isolde's, Seraphine's, Wren's. Each carries a different burden, a different resolve. The training is no longer about honing individual talents, but about preparing for survival, for sacrifice.
