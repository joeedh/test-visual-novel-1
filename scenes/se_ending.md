EXT. HALCYON REACH - DAY

[[nextline: 6]]

[[line: L1]]
Halcyon Reach, Seraphine's family seat, is bathed in soft, cloud-diffused silver light. The pale terraces and glass conservatories, once a place of tense diplomacy, now host a joyous celebration: our wedding.

[[line: L2]]
Seraphine stands beside me, radiant. She has chosen her own thread, a path forged in conviction and action, not the one spun for her by treaties and court expectations. The indigo-and-silver hangings of her house now symbolize her own, independent design.

CAEDON VALE
[[line: L3]]
You chose this, Seraphine. Your own truth. Not the peace they brokered, but the peace you fought for.

SERAPHINE HALCYON
[[line: L4]]
And in choosing, I found the only thread worth weaving. With you, Caedon. A chosen future.

[[line: L5]]
Among the allies she brokered, among the surviving pairs whose loyalty she earned, we exchange our vows. This is a union not just of two people, but of a vision for a new, more just world. The peace-broker's daughter, at last, truly free. The end.
