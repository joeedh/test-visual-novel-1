[[line: L1]]
Halcyon Reach, the family seat, is a place of pale terraces and glass conservatories, where wars are settled with words. Seraphine, my fierce diplomat, returns here not to mediate, but to act. Her blood and bearing open doors closed to everyone else, turning scandal into leverage, and leverage into a burgeoning coalition.

[[line: L2]]
While she moves through court chambers, exposing corruption with surgical precision, I work to gather intelligence, feeding her the evidence she needs. She weaves the fallout into powerful alliances, leveraging political intrigue and familial ties.

[[line: L3]]
We receive communications from other stranded pairs. Some have, out of desperation, assassinated their landing-nations' monarchs. Others build comms back to us, and Seraphine, with her unparalleled ability to connect and strategize, braids them into one formidable front. She is building a new kind of peace, forged not through diplomacy but through conviction.

[[line: L4]]
Her poise remains, but it's now underscored by a steely resolve. She is no longer merely playing the court's game; she is rewriting its rules, choosing her own thread in this grand tapestry of war and alliances.
