INT. ASHEN CIRCLE HAVEN - NIGHT

[[nextline: 5]]

[[line: L1]]
Deep beneath a dead furnace-works lies the Ashen Circle Haven, the beating heart of the underground. Here, the burned-out, the fugitive strong, and the Rust-touched shelter together, ungoverned and unbowed. Filed-smooth cogs hang everywhere as tokens of defiance. This is Wren's true home, a place no monarch commands.

[[line: L2]]
With Wren's guidance, we begin to build a coalition among the discarded. She opens doors in the smuggler-nets and among the dispossessed that no licensed official could ever access. Her value here is singular; she is the voice of the unheard.

[[line: L3]]
I work to expose corruption at the highest levels, feeding evidence into the intricate networks Wren threads. We receive word from surviving stranded pairs: some, desperate, assassinate rulers in their landing-nations; others open comms back to us, drawn by the promise of true freedom and the Ashen Circle's defiance.

[[line: L4]]
Our alliance grows, a mosaic of the world's forgotten and betrayed. Wren, usually so guarded, finds her voice among her own, leading with a quiet authority that inspires fierce loyalty. Together, we are preparing to take the fight to those who would harvest the innocent.
