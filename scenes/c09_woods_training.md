EXT. COLLEGE WOODS - DAY

[[next: c10_the_world_breaks]]
[[nextline: 7]]

[[line: L1]]
The College Woods, beyond the wards, offer a wild, unpoliced sanctuary. Dappled light filters through the canopy, and the Weave feels closer to the surface here, untamed. This is the first thing that is truly ours—or, for me alone, mine.

[[line: L2]]
(Ember path): Ember leads me to a mist-pooled clearing. "The College teaches metering," she says, a faint smile playing on her lips, the rust bloom on her arm a testament to her philosophy. "I'll teach you to spend. To burn. To live without counting the cost." She guides my hands, and I feel the raw current surge, unmetered, exhilarating. It is a shared defiance, an intimate lesson in power and abandon.

[[line: L3]]
(Isolde path): Isolde brings me to mossed standing stones, ancient and serene. Her voice is low, confiding. "They teach devotion, Caedon. But beneath it... there is doubt." She shows me subtle, precise weaves, ways to unknot and re-braid, not just power, but belief itself. She allows me to glimpse the vulnerability beneath her disciplined facade, and for the first time, I see the weight of her piety.

[[line: L4]]
(Seraphine path): Seraphine brings me to a hidden glade, where she braids Spirit and Air with unnerving delicacy. "The courts weave threads of obligation," she muses, her eyes distant. "Here, we learn to read each other's." We practice channeling in intricate, silent dances, learning to anticipate, to respond, to read the unspoken currents between us. It's a dance of trust, revealing the deep, complex patterns of her world.

[[line: L5]]
(Wren path): Wren leads me to a hidden bolt-hole, a small, camouflaged shelter deep in the woods. "Cloudlane craft," she murmurs, showing me how to jury-rig comms, how to read the subtle signs of the smuggler-nets. "The College won't teach you how to disappear." She's guarded, but here, in the quiet, she reveals the practical, brutal truths of her fugitive life. It is a shared secret, a bond forged in the shadows.

[[line: L6]]
(Undivided colour): I train alone, honing my senses, hardening my resolve. Each step is a measured exercise in control, in self-reliance. I practice channeling, not to bond, but to build myself into something unbreakable, something that can shoulder the weight of all that is to come. No shared intimacy, just the grim satisfaction of growing power. My self, not a bond, is building here.
