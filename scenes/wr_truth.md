INT. CLOUDLANE BOLTHOLE - NIGHT

[[nextline: 6]]

[[line: L1]]
High in a disused mooring-mast, tucked into the cloudlanes, is one of Wren's bolt-holes. It's cramped, filled with crates of contraband weaves, forged transit-tokens, and a filed-smooth cog nailed by the door—the defiant mark of the Ashen Circle. Wind moans through the girders, a lonely song.

[[line: L2]]
Threading the fugitive networks, Wren and I piece together the terrifying truth: the Assayers' program of mass severing isn't merely about policing ungoverned channelers. It's about *harvesting* them.

[[line: L3]]
The revelation hits hard. The College, the Governors, the entire system—it's all built on exploitation. Wren's face, usually so guarded, flickers with raw pain and outrage. This is the fate she has run from her entire life, now made chillingly real.

[[line: L4]]
Then, the news of the executed pair. It lands on us both like a hammer blow, but on Wren, it's a stark, personal terror. Exactly the fate she has fought to avoid, now a brutal reality for someone we knew. Her determination hardens, and mine with it.

[[line: L5]]
The air in the bolt-hole feels heavy with unspoken vows. We are no longer just surviving; we are fighting for the discarded, for those who cannot fight for themselves. Our path is set: to expose the harvest, to free the severed.
