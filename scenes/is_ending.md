The cold northern principality of Vorn is transformed, not by a softening of its iron will, but by a new, chosen purpose. Today, the grey stone devotional houses bear witness to a different kind of ceremony: our wedding.

Isolde stands beside me, her grey habit replaced by something softer, yet still imbued with her inherent grace. She has unclasped her armor, both literal and metaphorical, choosing her own thread, not the one spun for her by a rigid doctrine. Her key-pendant, once a symbol of the leash, now hangs as a testament to her freedom.

Among the people she has liberated from corruption, among those who now look to her for guidance, we exchange our vows. This is not just a union of two hearts, but a forging of a new path for Vorn, a path built on doubt turned to conviction, on devotion turned to justice. The end.
