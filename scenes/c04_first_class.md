The Channeling Lecture Hall hums with the low thrum of the great brass orrery of the Five Threads. Professor Quist stands by a diagram of current-flow, her expression stern but encouraging. Professor Thorn lurks in the shadows, a silent, judging presence.

PROFESSOR MIRABEL QUIST
[[line: L2]]
Today, we delve into the doctrine of the Weave. Metered power is safe power. Ungoverned channeling, as you know, is a crime the College itself trains you to police.

[[line: L3]]
I watch the love interests, observing their signature styles. Ember Kellan, refusing to meter, her Vash-strong current barely contained, scarred hands gloved. Isolde Vorn, leashing herself tight, Sela-strong precision almost painfully restrained. Seraphine Halcyon, braiding Spirit and Air with an unnerving, delicate control. Wren Ashfield, answering nothing she isn't forced to, her guarded strength simmering beneath the surface.

PROFESSOR SILAS THORN
[[line: L4]]
Observe. The difference between Vash-strong raw current and Sela-strong precision is not merely technique, but philosophy. Which do you choose to master?

[[line: L5]]
The question hangs in the air, a subtle challenge. My path is still unfolding, but the lines of conflict and allegiance are already being drawn.
