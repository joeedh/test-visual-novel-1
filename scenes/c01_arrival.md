EXT. COG COLLEGE APPROACH - DUSK

[[next: c02_first_meetings]]
[[nextline: 7]]

[[line: L1]]
The Cog-College. A gate, a viaduct, and a promise. The air smells of coal, hot brass, and the faint ozone tang of the Weave.

[[line: L2]]
Headmistress Coyle stands at the podium, her voice clear even over the hiss of steam vents.

HEADMISTRESS AURIA COYLE
[[line: L3]]
Welcome, new intake. You are the chosen, the metered, the licensed. This college will hone your talents.
[[line: L4]]
But know this: ungoverned power is a crime. The Weave demands discipline. Fail, and you will Rust. It is the college's mission to train you... and to police those who refuse its leash.

[[line: L5]]
I scan the crowd. Ember Kellan, fiery, a faint rust bloom already on her forearm. Isolde Vorn, still, aristocratic, clutching a key-pendant. Seraphine Halcyon, drawing eyes without trying, already surrounded by an entourage. Wren Ashfield, guarded, watching everyone from the periphery.

[[line: L6]]
My path begins here, among them, but I am yet to choose.
