[[line: L1]]
The Great Assembly Hall is filled with the entire cohort, the banners of the Orders hanging silently overhead. Headmistress Coyle stands at the rostrum, her voice echoing with the weight of her words.

HEADMISTRESS AURIA COYLE
[[line: L2]]
Students, you have proven yourselves capable in theory. Now, we test your application. You will be assigned to investigate a set of ancient ruins beyond the wards.
[[line: L3]]
This is not merely a practical exercise. The Weave within those ruins is unstable. You will work in teams, and your objective is to assess and report, not to engage. The stakes are real.

[[line: L4]]
My eyes drift across the hall, finding Ember, Isolde, Seraphine, and Wren among the assembled. The friend group—Gideon, Pip, Tobias—are also here, their faces a mix of apprehension and excitement.

[[line: L5]]
Teams are announced. I'm assigned with some of the friend group, and I notice each of the love interests are carefully distributed across other teams. A cold knot tightens in my stomach. The dangers to come are no longer theoretical.
