[[line: L1]]
The Foreign Court is a glittering, treacherous labyrinth of mirrored galleries and clockwork chandeliers. Every pleasantry is a move on a board, every envoy in their livery a player. We land here, Seraphine's name both a shield and a target, constantly on the run from security services.

[[line: L2]]
Seraphine navigates this world with breathtaking fluency. Her knowledge of court protocol and cipher is our only salvation. She opens sealed doors with a subtle phrase, reads a room's true allegiances at a glance, and weaves us through the diplomatic underlayer while danger constantly nips at our heels.

[[line: L3]]
Together, working in the shadows of opulent ballrooms, we uncover a chilling truth: the world's collapse is a coordinated design, not coincidence. Treaties, ostensibly for peace, were written to concentrate control of the Weave among powerful houses. The courts are complicit.

[[line: L4]]
The news of the executed pair hardens her. I see it in her eyes; the diplomat's gentle belief in negotiation is shattered. She stops believing she can broker her way out. Her resolve solidifies, replacing hope with a fierce determination to act. I follow her lead, knowing her path is now irrevocably her own.
