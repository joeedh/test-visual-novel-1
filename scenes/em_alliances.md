[[line: L1]]
Cinderhold, the Vashan Forge-City, glows with molten-orange light through a permanent haze of ember and soot. Great ungoverned aether-kilns roar without a Governor in sight, a testament to the Kiln-Sworn's defiance. Ember's ties run deep here; her name opens doors no licensed talent could ever hope to access.

[[line: L2]]
We meet with Kiln-Sworn contacts, their faces scarred with the marks of ungoverned power, but their eyes burning with loyalty. Ember's forge-craft, her knowledge of weapons and defenses, begins to arm and equip our growing coalition.

[[line: L3]]
While Ember builds her army, I expose corruption at the highest levels of the Dominion, feeding information into the burgeoning network. We hear reports of other stranded pairs—some, out of desperation, murdering the monarchs of their landing-nations; others, building communications back to us, drawn by the promise of Ember's defiance.

[[line: L4]]
Our coalition grows, fueled by a shared hunger for vengeance against the forces that shattered our world. Ember is in her element, but the danger of her recklessness weighs on me.
