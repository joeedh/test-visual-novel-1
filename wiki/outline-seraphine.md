# Route: Seraphine Halcyon

The diplomat's path. Caedon gravitates toward Seraphine Halcyon, the poised daughter of Halcyon Reach's ruling house, and the story's conflict is drawn from the tangle of courts, treaties, and the quiet war for who controls the Weave.

## first act

Caedon arrives at the Cog-College and meets his fellow students and teachers. He is drawn to Seraphine — silver-blonde, unhurried, watched by everyone — and finds himself charmed past her practiced court reserve. He meets the other male students and her circle of Loomwright-inclined peers.

Caedon goes to class. He sees Seraphine braid Spirit and Air with a delicacy that unnerves the instructors, and glimpses the peace-broker role her mother groomed her for chafing against her own hunger to choose her thread.

Caedon's friend group teams up to investigate the ruins as part of a test. Nefarious external actors intervene; Seraphine's diplomacy and perceptiveness turn a losing fight, and they emerge victorious — but she recognizes the intervention as no accident.

Caedon is drawn into the conflict surrounding Seraphine: envoys and rival houses courting Halcyon Reach, and a treaty that hides who truly means to leash the Weave.

## second act

Caedon goes training in the woods, Seraphine at his side, learning to read each other's threads.

Something goes wrong in the world. The school intensifies training. Caedon and his friends are teleported in pairs to random places around the world; Caedon is teleported with Seraphine — into a court far from home, where her name is both shield and target.

Caedon and Seraphine are on the run from hostile security services. Using her fluency in court and cipher, they discover the truth behind the world's collapse. Meanwhile another stranded pair, fleeing security forces in another country, is caught, interrogated, and publicly executed — a grief that hardens Seraphine's resolve to broker no more, but to act.

## third act

Caedon and Seraphine, with the remaining stranded pairs, begin seeking alliances. Seraphine's blood and bearing open doors closed to others; Caedon exposes corruption at the highest levels of governments. Some stranded pairs murder the monarchs of the nations they landed in; others build lines of communication back to Caedon, and Seraphine weaves them into a coalition.

Caedon and Seraphine infiltrate the organization behind the chaos. They fight several battles; the allies she brokered join them, and the remaining stranded pairs arrive to help fight the final boss.

Caedon marries Seraphine. The end.
