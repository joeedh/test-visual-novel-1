# Ember Kellan route

Caedon's route with Ember Kellan, the Kiln-Sworn forge-prodigy of the Vashan Dominion. This route's throughline is raw, ungoverned power against Ferran licensing — and the shadow of the Rust that will one day take her.

## first act

Caedon arrives at the Cog-College and, among the intake, is drawn to Ember Kellan — broad-shouldered, copper-haired, already rust-marked and unashamed. She channels raw current in the yards where others wouldn't dare, and her reckless brilliance both alarms and magnetizes him. He meets the other male students and Ember's uneasy place as the Dominion's test case: a heresy the Ferrans mean to tame.

Caedon goes to class. He sees Ember chafe against metered, licensed channeling, and glimpses the rust-scarring she wears like a dare.

Caedon's friend group teams up to investigate ruins as part of a test. When nefarious external actors intervene, Ember spends power without counting the cost — saving them, but burning herself in the process. They emerge victorious, and Caedon begins to understand what her recklessness costs her.

Caedon is drawn into the conflict around Ember: the Dominion's interest in her, the Ferran licensers' suspicion, and the question of whether ungoverned Vash strength can be controlled at all.

## second act

Caedon goes training in the woods, where Ember teaches him to spend rather than meter — a heresy that thrills and frightens him.

Something goes wrong in the world. The school intensifies training, then the students are teleported in pairs across the world. Caedon lands with Ember.

On the run from hostile security services, Ember's raw power is both their shield and a beacon that draws pursuit. They discover the truth behind the world's collapse. Meanwhile another stranded pair is caught, interrogated, and publicly executed — a fate Ember, who does not expect to live long, takes harder than she lets on.

## third act

Caedon and Ember, with the remaining stranded pairs, seek alliances. Ember's Kiln-Sworn ties and forge-craft open doors in the Vashan Dominion; Caedon exposes corruption while she arms their allies. Some stranded pairs murder the monarchs of the nations they landed in; others build lines of communication.

Caedon and Ember infiltrate the organization behind the chaos, fighting several battles in which she spends her strength recklessly, the Rust creeping further up her arm. Their recruited allies and the remaining stranded pairs join them for the final fight against the boss.

Caedon marries Ember, who loves fiercely because she never expected to love long. The end.
