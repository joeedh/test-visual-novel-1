# Route: Isolde Vorn

The route where Caedon's primary love interest is **Isolde Vorn**, daughter of the Grey Principality of Vorn, sent south to the Cog-Colleges to watch as much as to learn. The through-line is doubt against devotion: Isolde was raised where the Assayers leash channeling, and her cold, precise Sela strength wars with the piety she wears like armor.

## first act

Caedon arrives at the academy and meets his fellow students and teachers. He gravitates toward Isolde — drawn first by her aristocratic stillness, then by the sharp wit beneath the frost — and meets the other male students. Isolde keeps him at a careful distance; a Vorn daughter does not befriend ungoverned talent lightly.

Caedon goes to class. He sees how tightly Isolde leashes her own Weave, how her convictions flinch when confronted with power used freely.

Caedon's friend group teams up to investigate some ruins as part of a test. Nefarious external actors intervene and things go wrong. Isolde's disciplined, precise weaving saves them where recklessness would have failed — but to win she must break a rule she believes in, and it shakes her.

Caedon is drawn into the conflict surrounding Isolde: Vorn's watchers, the Assayers' long reach, and the question of whether she is a student or a spy for her province.

## second act

Caedon goes training in the woods. Isolde's certainty is cracking; she confides the doubt she has kept hidden, and lets him see the young woman aching to be more than her province's leash.

Something goes wrong in the world. The school intensifies training. The students are teleported in pairs to random places around the world — Caedon is teleported with Isolde.

Caedon and Isolde are on the run from hostile security services. Isolde's knowledge of Assayer methods and Vorn devotional networks keeps them alive; her key-pendant opens doors it should not. They discover the truth behind what has gone wrong in the world — a truth that implicates the very order Isolde was raised to trust. Meanwhile, one of the other stranded pairs is caught, interrogated, and publicly executed.

## third act

Caedon and Isolde, with the remaining stranded pairs, seek out alliances. Isolde's faith is now a weapon turned on its makers: she exposes corruption at the highest levels of the devout governments and the Assayer hierarchy. Some stranded pairs murder the monarchs or emperors of the nations they landed in; others build lines of communication with Caedon.

Caedon and Isolde infiltrate the organization behind the chaos. They fight several battles; the recruited allies and remaining pairs converge to help fight the final boss. Isolde chooses her own thread at last, her hidden knife of doubt now drawn in the open.

Caedon marries Isolde. The end.
