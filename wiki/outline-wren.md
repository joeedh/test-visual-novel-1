# Outline — Wren Ashfield route

The route where Caedon's primary love interest is Wren Ashfield, the burned-out
channeler and cloudlane smuggler of the Ashen Circle underground.

## first act

Caedon arrives at the Cog-College and, out of place among licensed talents, gravitates
toward Wren — a scholarship outsider who slips the College's leash as easily as breathing.
He meets the other male students. Wren is guarded; she assumes anyone official-looking will
sell her to the Grey Hands.

Caedon goes to class and sees how ungoverned channeling is punished. Wren hides her
Rust-scarred hands.

Caedon's friend group is sent to investigate ruins as part of a test. Nefarious external
actors intervene — and turn out to be Grey Hand Assayers hunting a fugitive, not students.
Wren's smuggling contacts are the only reason they escape; they emerge victorious but Wren
is now exposed.

Caedon is drawn into the conflict around Wren: the Assayers' hunt for severed channelers
and the Ashen Circle that shelters them.

## second act

Caedon goes training in the woods; Wren teaches him the smugglers' cloudlane craft the
College won't.

Something goes wrong in the world. The school intensifies training, then teleports the
students in pairs to random places. Caedon lands with Wren — in hostile territory where
her kind are shot on sight.

On the run from security services, Wren's underground contacts become their lifeline. They
discover the truth behind the chaos: it is tied to the Assayers' program of mass severing.
Meanwhile another stranded pair is caught, interrogated, and publicly executed.

## third act

Caedon and Wren, with the remaining pairs, seek alliances — Wren opening doors among the
discarded and the smuggler-nets no monarch controls. Caedon exposes corruption at the
highest levels. Some stranded pairs assassinate the rulers of the nations they landed in;
others build lines of communication back to Caedon.

Caedon and Wren infiltrate the organization behind the chaos, fighting several battles
with the Ashen Circle at their backs. Recruited allies and the surviving pairs converge for
the final fight.

Caedon marries Wren, who is finally trusted by someone who never sold her. The end.
