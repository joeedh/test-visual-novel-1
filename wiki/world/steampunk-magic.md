# The Aetherwork World

A soot-and-brass civilization risen on the back of the **Aether Engines** — vast
pressure furnaces that burn refined aether alongside coal. Airships ply cloudlanes
between city-states of iron viaducts, clocktower spires, and glass-domed
conservatories. Steam and steel are the visible power of the age; the invisible
power is the Weave.

## The Weave (the magic system)

Magic is the channeling of a single ambient force, the **Aether**, drawn through
a living conduit and shaped into effect. As in the Wheel of Time's One Power, the
source is one but divided.

### The Two Currents
- **Sela** — the ordered, structural current. Feels bright, geometric, cold.
- **Vash** — the wild, kinetic current. Feels hot, surging, alive.

Every channeler draws chiefly on one current; the two both oppose and complete
each other. Great works require them woven together, and the tension between them
is what gives a weave its strength — and its danger.

### The Five Threads
Raw current is shaped through five elemental Threads, braided into weaves:
**Fire, Air, Water, Stone, and Spirit**. Spirit is the subtlest and least
understood; most channelers are strong in only one or two Threads and must lean on
others to complete complex works.

### Channeling
To channel is to *surrender* to the current, not to seize it — the harder one
grasps, the more it slips. A channeler opens themselves, lets the Aether flow, and
guides rather than commands. Overreaching past one's capacity **burns out** the
conduit, leaving the channeler forever severed, or kills them outright.

### The Governors
Because raw channeling is perilous, the age's engineers built **Governors** —
brass-and-crystal regulators, worn as gauntlets or bolted into engine cores, that
meter and stabilize a channeler's flow. A Governor lets a weaker talent work safely
and lets industry harness the Weave at scale: aether furnaces are simply enormous
Governors tended by rings of bound channelers.

### The Taint
Generations ago, a catastrophic weaving cracked something in the Vash current.
Those who draw deeply on Vash risk the **Rust** — a slow corrosion of mind and
body, madness threaded with literal oxidation of the flesh. Sela remains clean.
This divide shapes every guild, law, and prejudice in the world.

## Institutions
- **The Cog-Colleges** — guild academies that test, train, and license channelers,
  and manufacture Governors.
- **The Assayers** — inspectors who hunt unlicensed channelers and Rust-touched.
- **The Ashen Circle** — an underground of the burned-out and the fugitive strong,
  who channel without Governors and pay the price.

## Open threads for stories
- Who cracked the Vash current, and can it be mended?
- A Governor that amplifies rather than meters — and who would kill for it.
- The first channeler in a generation strong in all five Threads.
