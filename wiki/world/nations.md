# Nations of the Aetherwork World

The cloudlanes and iron viaducts are carved up between two great powers and a
ring of smaller crowns. The empires burn aether at industrial scale and covet the
Orders of channelers; the monarchies survive by playing them against each other,
trading, or hoarding what the empires need.

## The Two Empires

### The Ferran Ascendancy
The elder empire, built on iron and law. Ferra is the heartland of the **Regulen
Concord** — every furnace metered, every channeler licensed, every citizen a cog
in a vast bureaucratic engine. Ruled by the **Iron Diet**, a council of guild-
lords beneath a ceremonial Ascendant. Ferra prizes order above all and views
ungoverned power as sedition. Its wealth is manufacture: it builds the world's
Governors and sells them dear. Colors: brass and slate. Capital: **Cograuncy**,
a terraced city of clockwork viaducts.

### The Vashan Dominion
The younger, hungrier empire, forged by conquest along the southern cloudlanes.
The Dominion embraces the **Kiln-Sworn** and their ungoverned Vash, fielding
battle-channelers the Ferrans will not. Ruled by a warrior-**Overlord** whose
throne passes to whoever can hold it. Dominion cities are furnace-forges wreathed
in ember-smoke, its airfleets the terror of the trade lanes. Colors: ash-grey and
ember-orange. Capital: **Cinderhold**, a fortress-city grown around a Rust-scarred
great furnace. Ferra and the Dominion are locked in a cold war of proxy and
embargo.

## The Five Monarchies

### Kingdom of Halcyon Reach
A cloud-shrouded highland realm of glass-domed conservatories and airship yards.
Wealthy, neutral, and the favored haven of the **Loomwrights**. Its queen brokers
peace between the empires — for a price. Colors: indigo and silver.

### The Marches of Cindral
A harsh volcanic border-kingdom, rich in raw aether ore, forever courted and
bullied by the Dominion next door. Its warrior-kings sell ore to the highest
bidder and keep their own Kiln-Sworn levies. Colors: crimson and black.

### The Saltbound Crownland
A maritime trade-monarchy of harbor-cities and lightning cloudlanes. Grows fat as
middleman between the empires and controls the choke-point straits. Its merchant-
king rules by ledger. Colors: sea-green and gold.

### The Grey Principality of Vorn
A cold northern realm, secretive and pious, where the **Assayers' Order** holds
unusual sway and channeling is tightly leashed. Ruled by a devout prince-regent.
Colors: unmarked grey and iron.

### The Free Duchy of Amberline
Smallest and richest per head — a canal-laced duchy of banks, artificers, and
spy-guilds. Owes nominal fealty to no empire and survives by knowing everyone's
secrets. Ruled by an elected Grand Duke and a merchant assembly. Colors: amber and
white.

## Fault lines
- **Ferra vs. the Dominion** — the cold war every monarchy is caught between.
- **Cindral's ore** — the resource both empires would go to war to control.
- **Halcyon's neutrality** — the diplomatic keystone; its fall would ignite the
  cloudlanes.
- **Vorn and the Assayers** — a realm that may become the Order's power base, or
  its cage.
