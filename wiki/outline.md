This is a generic template for story outlines.  The story branches depeneding on who the primary 
love interest is.  Assume for now the love interest doesn't change as the story goes on;
there will be four paths through the story.  

We will also eventually write a dark subversion of the polygamy harem game trope:
if Caedon doesn't fall for anyone he will in fact fall for all of them.  The story 
will branch at the point a stranded pair dies; Caedon will go on a dark path, eventually
using strategic-class weaves (kind of like nukes) to utterly destroy the organization 
behind the chaos in the world.  Along the way he will bloodily conquer one of the empires.
The story ends with a timeskip, where we see Caedon's many children fighting a bloody 
civil war that leaves the world in ruins.

# outline

## first act

Caedon arrives as the academy and meets his
fellow students and teachers.  He gravitates
towards a love interest and meets the other 
male students.  

Caedon goes to class.

Caedon and his friend group team up to investigate some ruins
as part of a test.  External nafarious external actors intervene
and things start to go wrong, but they manage to overcome and 
emerge victorious.

Caedon is drawn into whatever conflict surrounds the love interest.

## second act

Caedon goes training in the woods.

Something goes wrong in the world.  The school intensifies training.
Caedon and his friends are teleported to random places around the world
in pairs. Caedon is teleported with the love interest. 

Caedon and the love interests are on the run from hostile security services.
They discover the truth behind whatever's gone wrong in the world.  Meanwhile
one of the other stranded pairs are fleeing from security forces in another
country, they are caught and interrogated then publically executed.

## third act

Caedon and the love interest, along with the remaining other stranded pairs, 
begin seeking out alliances.  Caedon exposes corruption at the highest levels of 
governments.  Some stranded pairs work together to murder the monarchs or emperors
of the nations they landed in.  Still others work to build lines of communication
with Caedon.

Caedon and the love interest work together to infiltrate the organization behind 
the chaos in the world.  They fight several battles.  The allies they recruited join them.
The remaining stranded pairs also show up.  They all help fight the final boss.

Caedon marries the love interest.  The end.
