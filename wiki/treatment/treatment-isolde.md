# Treatment — Isolde Vorn Route

**Logline.** A devout daughter of the Grey Principality, sent south to spy as much as to study, discovers that the order she was raised to trust is the rot behind the world's collapse — and that the ungoverned talent she was taught to fear is the only thing worth believing in.

**Central conflict.** Doubt against devotion. Isolde's cold Sela precision is inseparable from the piety that leashes it; Caedon's route is the slow, painful unclasping of that armor until she chooses her own thread.

## Act One — The Watcher

Caedon arrives at the Cog-College among licensed talents and is drawn to Isolde Vorn: an aristocrat's stillness, a devout's certainty, and a wit sharp enough to cut through both. She keeps him at arm's length — a Vorn daughter does not befriend ungoverned talent lightly, and she is here to watch as much as to learn.

In class, Caedon sees how tightly Isolde leashes her own Weave, and how her convictions flinch whenever power is used freely. Her key-pendant marks her as more than a student.

The friend-group ruins test goes wrong when nefarious actors intervene. Isolde's disciplined, precise weaving saves them where recklessness would have failed — but winning forces her to break a rule she genuinely believes in, and the transgression shakes her to the core.

Caedon is pulled into the conflict around her: Vorn's watchers, the Assayers' long reach, and the open question of whether Isolde is a student or a spy.

## Act Two — The Cracking

Woods training. Isolde's certainty is fracturing; she confides the doubt she has hidden all her life and lets Caedon glimpse the young woman aching to be more than her province's leash.

The world breaks. The school intensifies training, then teleports the students in pairs. Caedon lands with Isolde.

On the run from security services, Isolde's knowledge of Assayer methods and Vorn devotional networks keeps them alive — her key-pendant opens doors it should not. They uncover the truth behind the collapse, and it implicates the very order Isolde was raised to serve. Another stranded pair is caught, interrogated, and publicly executed; the news lands on Isolde like a verdict on her own faith.

## Act Three — The Knife Drawn

With the remaining pairs, Caedon and Isolde seek alliances. Her faith becomes a weapon turned on its makers: she exposes corruption at the highest levels of the devout governments and the Assayer hierarchy. Some stranded pairs assassinate the rulers of their landing-nations; others open lines of communication back to Caedon.

Caedon and Isolde infiltrate the organization behind the chaos, fighting several battles as their recruited allies and the surviving pairs converge for the final fight. Isolde, at last, draws her hidden knife of doubt in the open and chooses her own thread.

Caedon marries Isolde. The end.
