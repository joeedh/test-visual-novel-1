Scrubbed open country on the marches of the Vashan Dominion — red dust, wind-carved rock, dead telegraph lines, and no forge for a hundred miles to shelter a Kiln-Sworn. The horizon is too wide to hide in; every use of raw current throws a beacon across it. It is where Ember and Caedon land far from any friendly kiln, her power both their only shield and the thing that keeps drawing pursuit down on them. Here Ember lets slip how little time she believes the Rust will leave her, and the executed pair lands on Caedon as a warning about her.
