The Halcyon family seat, a Loomwright-inclined house of pale terraces and glass conservatories perched above the cloudlanes, indigo-and-silver thread in every hanging. Far-speaking looms and treaty-chambers make it a place where wars are settled with words. Groomed to broker peace here, Seraphine returns having chosen to act rather than mediate — braiding scandal into a single coalition of allies and surviving pairs. It is here that she, at last, chooses her own thread rather than the one spun for her. The route's wedding is held on its cloud-terraces.
