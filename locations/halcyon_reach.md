The Halcyon family seat, a Loomwright-inclined house of pale terraces and glass conservatories perched above the cloudlanes, indigo-and-silver thread in every hanging. Far-speaking looms and treaty-chambers make it a place where wars are settled with words. Groomed to broker peace here, Seraphine returns having chosen to act rather than mediate — braiding scandal into a single coalition, and, at the last, choosing her own thread. The route's wedding is held on its cloud-terraces.
