A cathedral-sized engine-vault housing the college's translocation array: nested brass rings twenty feet across, wound with crystal conduits and tended by a ring of bound channelers at their Governors. When it fires it floods the hall with searing teal aether-flare and a bone-deep thrum. Built for careful, one-pair transit, it is pushed here to breaking — students hustled onto the plate in pairs and flung to random corners of the world as the crisis closes in. The last safe place, used in a moment that is anything but.
