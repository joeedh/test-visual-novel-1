The underground's beating heart: a hidden warren beneath a dead furnace-works where the burned-out, the fugitive strong, and the Rust-touched shelter together, ungoverned and unbowed. Filed-smooth cogs hang everywhere as tokens; salvaged Governors are stripped for forbidden work; a communal fire keeps the discarded warm. No monarch commands the doors Wren opens here. It is where the coalition of the thrown-away is built — and where her wedding, among people who never sold her, is finally held.
