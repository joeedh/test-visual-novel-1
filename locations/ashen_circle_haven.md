The underground's beating heart: a hidden warren beneath a dead furnace-works where the burned-out, the fugitive strong, and the Rust-touched shelter together, ungoverned and unbowed. This is a haven for the discarded, where filed-smooth cogs hang everywhere as tokens of defiance, and salvaged Governors are stripped for forbidden work. A communal fire keeps the ungoverned warm. No monarch commands the doors Wren opens here; it is where the coalition of the thrown-away is built, and where her wedding, among people who never sold her, is finally held.
