The seat of Assayer power in Vorn: a fortress-chapter house of grey basalt and cold iron, its great door marked with the single iron key of the Grey Hands. Vaults hold rows of Nullifier Governors and the ledgers of the severed; the air is incense and machine-oil at once. This is where corruption reaches highest and where Isolde turns her faith into a weapon against its makers — exposing corruption at the highest levels of the devout governments and Assayer hierarchy, and walking in on a devotion she no longer owes.
