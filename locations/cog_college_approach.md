The outer approach to the Cog-College: a long iron viaduct climbing to gatehouses of brass and soot-darkened stone, geared Regulen sigils worked into the ironwork. Clocktower spires and glass-domed conservatories rise beyond a portcullis of interlocking cogs, and airship mooring-masts prick the skyline where cloudlane traffic drifts in. Steam vents hiss from grates underfoot; the air smells of coal, hot brass, and the faint ozone tang of the Weave. This is where the metered, licensed intake first gathers, a threshold built to remind every new talent exactly whose rules they are entering.
