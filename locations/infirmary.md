A long white ward of iron cots and glass cabinets, its air kept still and clean by a metered filtration weave. Copper burn-salves, Loomwright healing-threads, and Nullifier cuffs for the dangerously overdrawn sit ready on wheeled trays. Rust-scarring is treated here quietly, and never without a report filed. It is where the cost of a victory shows up on the body, and where words too raw for the courtyard finally get said in the aftermath of the ruins ambush.
