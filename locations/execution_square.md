id: execution_square
name: Execution Square
---

A public plaza built for the display of state power: a broad flagged square hemmed by government facades, a raised iron scaffold at its heart, broadcast-horns and scrying-plates mounted high so the whole quarter can be made to watch. Assayer grey and governmental brass. Crowd-barriers, banners of a hostile regime, and the cold theatrical lighting of a place designed so that punishment is never private. In daylight it is grimly ordinary; lit for an execution it becomes the stage on which the story's cruelty is made undeniable.
