A nowhere-country of wind-scoured heath and broken drystone walls, the kind of blank between-nations margin the College's panic-teleport flings its students into: no forge, no court, no warren, no cog-token that means anything here. Grey scudding cloud, a horizon of low hills and the rusted skeleton of some abandoned relay-mast. Cold, exposed, and anonymous — a place to be hunted across or to huddle out of the wind while a scrying-plate or a stolen broadcast-horn brings terrible news from somewhere else. It wears the palette of ash and iron and dead bracken. This is where students land after being scattered in pairs, and where Caedon may learn of the executed pair.
