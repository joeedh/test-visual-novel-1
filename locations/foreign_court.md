A gilded court of a rival city-state: mirrored galleries, clockwork chandeliers, and antechambers where every pleasantry is a move on a board. Envoys in the liveries of half the Orders circle sealed doors that open only to the right phrase. For Seraphine the place is both shield and target — her name known in every alcove — and her fluency in court protocol and cipher is the only thing that lets her and Caedon work its underlayer while security services hunt them through the ballrooms.
