A field of collapsed aetherwork on a wind-scoured moor beyond the cloudlanes: toppled clocktower drums, a fallen furnace-spine gone green with corrosion, viaduct arches broken off into open sky. Old Governors lie half-buried and cold, and a faint teal residue still weeps from cracked conduits where a great weaving once failed. Silent but for wind and the tick of settling metal, it is exactly the kind of place a field-test is set — and exactly the kind of place an ambush hides.
