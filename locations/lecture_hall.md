A steep-raked theatre of dark wood benches descending to a demonstration floor, where a great brass orrery of the Five Threads turns slowly above a warded dais. Diagrams of Governors and current-flow hang between tall windows; a wall of confiscated, filed-smooth cogs serves as a quiet warning about ungoverned channeling. Here the doctrine is taught as much as the craft — measured power is safe power — and the contrast between Sela precision and Vash current is put on the floor for all to see.
