The beating hub of the college: a broad flagstone quad ringed by lecture wings, dormitory stacks, and the towering aether-furnace hall whose vent-stacks breathe a soft teal shimmer over everything. A central fountain runs on a slow Governor-metered weave, water lifting and folding in impossible geometric arcs. Students in brass-and-white Regulen livery cross between classes; noticeboards clatter with reassignment cogs. It feels ordered, watched, and faintly performative — a place designed to be seen behaving well. It is where Caedon first crosses paths with the four love interests, each introduced in their element.
