The fortified outer works of the organization behind the world's collapse: black-iron curtain walls, gun-galleries, and vast aether-furnace stacks bleeding a sickly green glow into a permanent industrial dusk. Nullifier pylons ring the approach to clamp shut any weave. This is where the converging allies and surviving stranded pairs fight their way in — the set-piece battlefront shared by every route's endgame, and where the infiltration battles take place across the romance branches.
