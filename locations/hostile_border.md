A militarized frontier where the unlicensed and Rust-touched are shot on sight: checkpoint towers, razor-cog wire, propaganda hoardings, and Assayer patrols working the rail-yards with Nullifier cuffs. Wren's gloved hands and scars mark her instantly here. It is where the roles invert — where Caedon's schooling is worthless and only Wren's underground, her bolt-holes and forged tokens and called-in debts, keeps them both breathing. This is where they land, and where the stark reality of Wren's life as a fugitive is immediately apparent.
